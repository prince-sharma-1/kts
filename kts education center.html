O level

The NIELIT (formerly DOEACC) 'O' Level course is a one-year, government-recognized foundational certification in Information Technology, aimed at equipping students with practical IT skills. It covers topics like programming, web design, and IT tools, preparing learners for roles like Web Designer or IT Assistant, often equivalent to a Foundation Level Course.

A level

A-levels (Advanced Levels) are two-year, subject-based qualifications for students aged 16–18, typically taken after GCSEs in the UK and internationally to prepare for university. Usually studying three or four subjects, students gain in-depth knowledge, with results determining university admission via UCAS points. Popular subjects include Mathematics, Sciences, Psychology, and English.
