## How to use this list

Start with Brij Murti Palace, Paradise Grand, Vidhi Interiors, Smart Decor and Madhav Hotel. First two have published text issues recorded in the original audit; the other three are service-fit hypotheses. Do not assume any business needs a new website or has a marketing budget. For directory-only entries, verification is the next task, not an immediate sales pitch. A missing phone below means it was not verified, not that the business has no phone. Directory phones can be call-routing numbers. Do not use a marketplace's central booking/sales helpline as the vendor's number.

Suggested offers are discovery angles, not findings. Websites already exist for several candidates. Pitch a rebuild only after a specific need is established. Salons need image permissions and footage; filming is outside our base scope. Photography studios may be stronger referral partners than retainer buyers. Partner conversations do not count toward revenue until a paid contract exists.

## Outreach sequence

1. Review five candidates daily; identify current official business contact or verify locally.
2. Note one specific observed fact and ask a short question about their needs.
3. Log identity verification, date/channel, response and next action in the existing prospect tracker.
4. Stop on refusal; use the existing two-follow-up limit otherwise.
5. Qualify current marketing arrangement, usable assets, approval owner, budget and timing before a proposal.

Duplicates consolidated: P N/PN Furniture, repeated Shalu Furniture listings, Vidhi Interiors/LLP and R D Studio variants. Similar names are not proof of common ownership; confirm identities before outreach. No listing ratings, prices, revenue, market saturation or willingness to buy have been treated as evidence of lead quality.
