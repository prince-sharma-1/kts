## Offer 1 — Social Presence

**₹15,000 per 30-day cycle.** For coaching businesses that need consistent, clear content using their existing photos and footage.

Included: 8 static/carousel posts, 4 simple videos up to 30 seconds each edited from supplied footage, captions, a monthly calendar, scheduling on Instagram and Facebook, and one monthly report. Content is adapted across both platforms; this does not mean 12 separate pieces for each platform. Carousels have up to 5 slides. Two consolidated revision rounds per monthly batch.

Client provides approved course details, logos, images, usable footage and necessary account permissions. Agree the production calendar after checking inputs and capacity. Scheduling starts after approval.

Not included: filming, paid advertising, daily inbox/community management, influencer costs or guaranteed admissions/leads. Fee is paid before the cycle starts. Confirm any applicable taxes explicitly in the proposal; do not imply an unconfirmed tax status.

Conversation version: “We can help your institute show up consistently with 8 posts and 4 short videos each month, using your photos and footage. The fee is ₹15,000 per 30-day cycle, including captions, scheduling and a simple report.”

## Offer 2 — Business Website

**₹25,000 fixed project.** For coaching businesses that need a clear, mobile-friendly place to explain their courses and receive enquiries.

Included: up to 5 informational pages, responsive layout, basic copy preparation, page titles/descriptions, contact and WhatsApp links, two consolidated revision rounds and a 14-day post-handover bug-fix window. Suggested pages: Home, Courses, About, FAQs and Contact.

Client supplies accurate course information, approved images/branding and access where needed. Domain and hosting are purchased by the client separately. No e-commerce, custom backend, login, payment gateway or ongoing maintenance is included. An enquiry database/form backend requires a separate scope.

Payment: ₹15,000 advance; ₹10,000 after agreed staging acceptance and before launch/handover. Target 10 working days after complete inputs, subject to confirmed capacity and timely approvals.

Conversation version: “We can build a focused website that explains your courses and makes it easy for parents and students to contact you. Up to five pages costs ₹25,000; domain and hosting are separate.”

## Choose prospects before writing messages

Prioritise a business with a relevant course offer, an identifiable owner/decision maker, a public business contact and one verifiable website/content issue. Check current official pages. Record the source and date. Do not confuse an inactive profile with a closed business or invent commercial performance.

Good observations: a course page lacks current batch information; a contact link is broken; the mobile page makes course details difficult to read. “Your marketing is bad” is not a useful observation. Never claim an issue unless checked.

## First introduction — customise before sending

Hi [name], I’m [founder name] from KTS Digital Marketing. I was looking at [institute]'s [website/public page] and noticed [specific verified observation]. We help coaching businesses present their courses clearly through websites and social content. Would a 15-minute conversation about your current needs be useful?

### If they ask for details

We offer monthly social content at ₹15,000 and business websites at ₹25,000, with a defined scope. Which is more relevant right now: keeping your social pages consistent or improving how people find course information and contact you?

### Warm introduction request

Hi [name], I’ve started KTS Digital Marketing, helping coaching institutes with websites and social content. Do you know an owner who might find that useful? If so, would you be comfortable introducing us? Please check with them before sharing a personal number.

### Follow-up after 3–4 working days

Hi [name], following up on my note about [specific topic]. If improving [website/course communication] is a current priority, I’d be happy to discuss it briefly. If the timing isn’t right, no problem.

One further relevant follow-up about a week later is enough. Stop if declined. Do not send every template at once or use unverified observations.

### If asked for previous results

KTS Digital Marketing is a new venture. I can show you clearly labelled concept work and explain exactly what we would deliver. I won't present sample work as a past client result.

## 15-minute discovery call

1. Minutes 0–3: Which courses/services matter most, and who is the buyer?
2. Minutes 3–6: How do enquiries arrive today, and who follows them up?
3. Minutes 6–9: What is the specific problem: course clarity, consistency, website usability or something else?
4. Minutes 9–12: What assets, budget, timeline and approval process are available? Who decides?
5. Minutes 12–15: Summarise the need and agree the next step. Propose only relevant work you can deliver.

Call notes: business / decision maker / problem / current approach / desired outcome / available assets / budget / timeline / approvals / agreed next action.

Do not promise enrolments or guaranteed leads. A social content package is not a paid lead-generation campaign.

## Before sending a proposal

- The prospect has a real need that matches one offer.
- You have confirmed scope, inputs, timing and available delivery capacity.
- Fees, payment milestones, exclusions and revision limits are written down.
- Any third-party costs and applicable taxes are explicitly addressed.
- Sample work is labelled as a concept; no invented testimonials or results appear.
- Proposal ends with one clear next step and a date for follow-up.

Record actual prospects researched, introductions sent, replies and calls booked. A non-response is not a rejection or a qualified lead. Set the next action for each prospect. Tomorrow's asset target is three clearly labelled concept social posts.
