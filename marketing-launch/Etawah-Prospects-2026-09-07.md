1. Brij Murti Palace: specific published information conflict; lead with a useful clarification, not an allegation or a full rebuild.
2. Paradise Grand: specific naming inconsistency; first establish business identity and whether events matter.
3. Vidhi Interiors: local showroom and product range fit the existing social offer; need remains a hypothesis.
4. Smart Decor: Etawah branch is explicitly listed; first establish local decision authority.
5. Madhav Hotel: published wedding/event offer fits venue content; content availability and need must be checked.

Only the first two have specific text issues identified. The other three are discovery candidates chosen for service fit and published business contact availability. This ranking does not estimate ability to pay. If five-way sector comparison matters more, substitute V3 for Madhav after confirming its booking contact belongs to the studio.

## Qualification and next action

Confirm the business identity and correct decision maker. Ask whether they already work with an agency, what is missing, whether usable approved media exists, their budget, and who approves. Only then discuss ₹15,000 per 30-day social cycle with the existing scope. A website correction is a separately scoped task, not automatically the ₹25,000 new-site package. Do not offer free production, imply affiliation, promise bookings or suggest they replace a working agency without a reason.
